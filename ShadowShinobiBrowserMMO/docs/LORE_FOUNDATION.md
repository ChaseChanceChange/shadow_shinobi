# Shadow Shinobi Lore Foundation

## Design objective

Shadow Shinobi is a browser MMORPG about covert operatives, competing enclaves, dangerous frontiers, and the struggle to master a supernatural force called Essence.

The first lore pass intentionally stays compact. It supplies enough fiction to explain the existing game systems without requiring a new engine.

## World premise

The old world was connected by stable roads, trade routes, and powerful settlements. That order fractured after a period now called **the Veiling**.

During the Veiling, regions became difficult to cross, old settlements were isolated, and strange concentrations of Essence appeared across the frontier. Some people learned to manipulate that force through disciplined techniques known as Arts.

The result is a world where information, movement, trained operatives, and recovered artifacts matter as much as raw strength.

## Player role

Players are independent operatives attached to an Enclave but capable of travelling outside its borders.

A player starts with limited Standing, basic Gear, and a small number of Arts. They build capability by training, accepting Contracts, exploring, recovering resources, defeating Threats, and interacting with other operatives.

The long-term objective is deliberately broader than reaching a single named title:

> Build enough Standing to influence the balance between the Enclaves and determine what the world becomes after the Veiling.

This supports future faction, PvP, and narrative systems without forcing the current engine into a single linear story.

## Enclaves

The initial world uses five major locations as the foundation for the legacy town grid. Their names and final political identities are provisional.

1. **Blackleaf Enclave** — trade, administration, and novice contracts.
2. **Spirit Mountain** — dangerous terrain and advanced Essence disciplines.
3. **Stonecrest Enclave** — military discipline and formal Challenges.
4. **Mistveil Enclave** — intelligence, scouting, and covert operations.
5. **Red Dune Enclave** — frontier commerce, dangerous recovery work, and refinement.

Additional settlements can be introduced later without changing the current map model.

## Essence

Essence is the world's supernatural energy. It is not treated as a simple mana replacement in the fiction.

Essence can be:

- channelled through Arts;
- condensed into specialized Gear;
- detected in places where the Veiling was especially strong;
- recovered from unusual Threats or artifacts;
- used in Refinement.

This gives future content a common explanation for abilities, items, alchemy/refinement, unusual enemies, and exploration discoveries.

## Arts

Arts are learned procedures that manipulate the body, Gear, environment, or Essence.

They are intentionally broader than "spells". An Art might be a strike, defensive maneuver, recovery technique, tracking method, movement technique, or other specialized action.

Legacy ability records can therefore be reused while their names, descriptions, costs, and fictional explanations are rewritten.

## Contracts

Contracts are jobs accepted by operatives.

The current quest/mission mechanics can represent:

- elimination;
- recovery;
- escort or protection;
- reconnaissance;
- delivery;
- investigation;
- training assignments.

The engine does not need a new mission framework for the first content pass. The narrative layer supplies the variety.

## Threats and Recovery

Creatures, hostile operatives, and other hazards are collectively treated as Threats.

Defeating a Threat can produce Recovery: equipment, materials, currency, or other useful objects.

This terminology lets the existing drop system remain intact while avoiding franchise-specific language.

## Progression

The legacy numeric level system is initially presented to players as **Standing**.

Standing represents a mixture of reputation, training, survivability, and operational experience. The existing experience and level mathematics remain unchanged during the first retheme pass.

This is deliberately a presentation-layer interpretation rather than a mechanical rewrite.

## Tone

Shadow Shinobi should feel:

- mysterious without becoming grimdark;
- practical rather than melodramatic;
- grounded in covert work and travel;
- respectful of the player's intelligence;
- capable of humor in NPC dialogue;
- expandable for both serious and playful content.

Avoid direct references to existing anime characters, named villages, trademarked terminology, or copied plot events.

## Canon rules for future content

New content should answer four questions:

1. What does this object/place/character do mechanically?
2. Why does it exist in the Shadow Shinobi world?
3. Which existing system does it attach to?
4. Does its wording depend on any legacy franchise concept?

If the answer to question 4 is yes, rewrite the fiction before publishing it.

## AI authoring rule

Any AI generating Shadow Shinobi content must preserve the engine-facing contract while treating the lore as original.

AI-generated content must not introduce a character, faction, place, item, ability, or story event by importing a recognizable Naruto character or franchise-specific setting and merely changing the spelling.

When uncertain, prefer a new description built from the established Shadow Shinobi concepts of Enclaves, Essence, Arts, Contracts, Threats, Recovery, and the Veiling.
