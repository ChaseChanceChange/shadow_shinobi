# Shadow Shinobi Starter Lore

## Core premise

The world is recovering from **the Veiling**, a historical rupture that disrupted travel, separated settlements and changed how Essence behaves in the frontier.

Players are independent **Operatives** working with an **Enclave**. They take **Contracts**, develop **Discipline**, master **Arts**, explore dangerous regions and recover useful materials from **Threats**.

The current game systems do not need a new engine to support this fiction.

## Starting situation

The player begins near **Blackleaf Enclave**, a practical trade settlement built around a surviving route through the Veiled frontier.

Something is wrong with that route. Reports describe:

- travellers disappearing between known coordinates;
- Threats carrying unusually concentrated Essence;
- damaged markers appearing where no settlement should exist;
- old Recovery items becoming active when brought near certain places.

The player is initially asked to investigate small, low-risk Contracts. The early game therefore teaches movement, combat, Gear, Pack management and Discipline while quietly introducing the larger mystery.

## Starter arc: The Broken Marker

### Contract 1 — Missing Courier

A courier failed to reach Blackleaf Enclave. The player searches nearby coordinates and defeats a small Threat.

**Purpose:** tutorial for exploration and combat.

### Contract 2 — Unmarked Trail

The courier's last known position contains a damaged route marker that does not match any current map record.

**Purpose:** tutorial for investigation and map discovery.

### Contract 3 — Residual Essence

A recovered object from the site reacts to Essence. A local specialist asks the player to bring it to a Settlement for examination.

**Purpose:** tutorial for Recovery and Refinement.

### Contract 4 — The Second Signal

The marker responds to another object somewhere beyond the normal beginner area. Reaching it requires stronger Standing and better Gear.

**Purpose:** create the first reason to progress beyond the starting region.

### Contract 5 — The Veil Remembers

The player learns that the markers predate the current Enclave borders and may be part of a network built before the Veiling.

**Purpose:** open the wider campaign without forcing a single faction or endgame path.

## Tone

Keep early dialogue short and readable. The setting should feel mysterious because the player discovers information through play rather than because every NPC speaks in riddles.

Humour is allowed, particularly from practical NPCs who are tired of adventurers making obvious mistakes.

## Content reuse strategy

Existing mission structures can represent these Contracts. Existing enemy rows can receive new names and descriptions. Existing reward/drop mechanics can remain unchanged while the fictional explanation is rewritten.

New lore should be layered over the existing gameplay rather than requiring a replacement engine.

## Canon constraint

Do not use recognizable names, characters, villages, clans, tailed creatures, signature techniques or plot events from unrelated franchises. Shadow Shinobi content should stand on its own.
